INCOMING FROM: THE LANTERN COLLECTIVE

They can erase a district.
Don’t let them erase its people.

At 03:17, the city’s last community archive goes dark. One damaged packet survives: recorded voices of people whose homes vanished behind a redevelopment wall.

The Lantern Collective has a receiver waiting. You have three network hops to get the recording to them. Quiet routes damage the file. Clean channels leave a trail.

YOUR OBJECTIVE

Deliver the recording with as much integrity—and as little exposure—as you can.

No timer. No account. Choose at your own pace.

02 / BEHIND THE SIGNAL

A small system with something to say.

THE WORLD
MADE ME CURIOUS.

Cyberpunk 2077 made me care about the people inside a complicated system. This is my own small exploration of that idea.

GHOST SIGNAL is a personal dedication to CD PROJEKT RED: an original scene, a handful of decisions, and an interface that lets you feel their consequences. The aim is simple—give a visitor something to play, then make the design and code understandable.

01 / DESIGN

Every choice has a cost.

Each route shows its exact effect before you commit. Exposure and integrity pull in different directions, making the tradeoff legible without a tutorial or a countdown.

02 / ENGINEERING

One state. One story.

A small state machine advances through three stages. Every choice updates the same values, selected SVG connections, event log, and final outcome. There is no random result.

03 / EXPERIENCE

Built for a real browser.

Native buttons, visible keyboard focus, live status updates, reduced-motion support, and a layout that works on a phone. Local fonts and no runtime dependencies keep the experience self-contained.

Read the outcome rules +

The simulation starts at 12% exposure and 72% integrity. Each choice adds or removes the amount shown; values stay between 0 and 100.

  • Exposure ≥ 65%: the packet is traced, regardless of its integrity.
  • Otherwise, integrity ≥ 85%: the complete recording arrives safely.
  • Otherwise, integrity < 58%: only a fragment of the recording survives.
  • Otherwise: a partial but usable recording arrives quietly.

There are 27 possible routes and four possible endings. Replay to explore how a different decision changes the result.
